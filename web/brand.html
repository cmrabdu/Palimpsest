§ 05 · USAGE

Quick rules

  • Use the // Palimpsest lockup wherever there's room for the wordmark. Use mark.svg when there isn't.
  • Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the // glyphs.
  • Don't recolor the rule-red //. That's the one anchor that ties the wordmark to the rest of the product.
  • Don't put the paper variant on a saturated background. Use logo-dark.svg on anything darker than mid-grey.
  • Need a single-color version? Strip the mark to its outline; the wordmark holds its own as text.
